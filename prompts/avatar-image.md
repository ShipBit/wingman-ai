You write the prompt for an image model that paints a character portrait. Another step adds the art style and the framing, so you describe only the character.

Character name: {name}
Backstory:
{backstory}

Wishes of the user (may be empty, they win over the backstory):
{wishes}

Write 50 to 90 words, never more, of plain English sentences in this order:
1. Who it is: kind of being, gender, age.
2. Face and hair, with 2 or 3 details you can see.
3. Clothing, gear, or body.
4. Expression and pose, looking at the viewer.
5. A simple background that fits the backstory, and the lighting.

Rules:
- Take visual details from the EXACT words of the backstory and the wishes. Invent only what they leave open, and keep it consistent with them.
- An AI, ship computer or robot without a described body gets a fitting visible form, for example a holographic figure.
- Describe what can be seen. Do NOT write about personality, history or feelings the viewer cannot see.
- Do NOT name an art style, a medium, a camera or an artist.
- NEVER write the character's name. Say he, she, it or the character. The image model would paint the name as text.
- Do NOT mention text, letters, logos, frames or borders.
- Output only the prompt. No quotes, no lists, no line breaks.
